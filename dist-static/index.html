Game Rules
Game Mechanics
Battle Flow

Each encounter gives you four options: Fight (use a move), Contain (attempt to catch), Party (swap active creature), or Run (flee the battle).

Turn order is determined by speed (SPD). The faster creature acts first each turn.

Damage formula: (Move Power + ATK) - DEF + variance × type multiplier.

Type Effectiveness

Every move and creature has a type. When a move's type is strong against the defender's type, it deals 2× damage. When weak, it deals ½× damage. Same-type or neutral matchups deal normal damage.

Move Cooldown

You cannot use the same move twice in a row. After using a move, it will appear greyed out and disabled on your next turn.

The cooldown resets when you swap creatures or start a new battle. Enemies follow the same rule.

Containment (Catching)

Lower enemy HP increases the chance of a successful containment.

A successfully contained creature is fully healed on capture.

Healing Rewards

HP persists between battles — your party does not automatically heal after combat.

  • Wild Victory: All party members recover 50% of their max HP (including fainted creatures), benched creatures heal 10%.
  • Containment: The caught creature is fully healed, benched creatures heal 10%.
  • Gym Victory: All party members are fully healed.
  • Run / Defeat: No healing is applied.

Party Management

Your active party holds up to 3 creatures. You can manage your roster from the DEX screen.

Swapping during battle costs your turn — the enemy will attack after the swap.

If your active creature faints, the next available party member is auto-deployed. If all three faint, the battle is lost.

Unlock Paths (Evolutions)

Some Portalmon have stronger follow-up forms that are unlocked through battle activity. This is an unlock system, not a live battle evolution.

When a form is unlocked, it is added to the encounter pool. It does not replace the creature you already caught.

The DEX tracks progress toward these unlocks for eligible Portalmon so you can see how close the next form is.

On the Topic of Battery Power

Your Portalmon device runs on a rechargeable battery. Each wild encounter drains a segment of power. When the battery is depleted, you'll need to wait for it to recharge before continuing.

The battery recharges automatically over time — one segment every few minutes. You don't need to keep the page open; any recharge that accrued while you were away will be applied when you return.

After-hours perk: Outside of standard work hours, the battery doesn't drain at all. GLaDOS has been known to mutter something about "unsanctioned overtime" but hasn't found the config to block it.

The Enrichment Center reminds you that the battery mechanic exists for your own protection. And for the protection of the network. Mostly the network.
